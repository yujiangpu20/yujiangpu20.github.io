## About Me

I'm a thrid-year Ph.D. student in the <a href="https://www.cse.msu.edu/" target="_blank"> Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)</a> at <a href="https://msu.edu/" target="_blank"> Michigan State University (MSU)</a>, advised by Prof.<a href="https://www.egr.msu.edu/~yukong/" target="_blank"> Yu Kong</a> in the <a href="http://aiactionlab.com/" target="_blank"> ACTION Lab</a>. Previously, I received my Master's degree (2020-2023) and Bachelor's degree (2016-2020) from the <a href="https://ices.cuc.edu.cn" target="_blank"> School of Information and Communication Engineering</a>, <a href="https://www.cuc.edu.cn" target="_blank"> Communication University of China (CUC)</a>, under the guidance of Prof. <a href="https://ices.cuc.edu.cn/2019/0919/c5332a135735/page.htm" target="_blank"> Xiaoyu Wu</a> at the State Key Laboratory of Media Convergence and Communication (MCC).

I am engaged in research in **Computer Vision (CV)** and **Machine Learning (ML)**, particularly in the area of **vision-language perception** and **generation**. My ultimate research goal is to enable intelligent systems to **perceive**, **reason**, and **predict** in complex visual environments. Specifically, my current research interests lie in:
<ul style="margin:0 0 20px;">
  <li><strong>Controllable Video Generation:</strong> Developing controllable text/image-to-video frameworks, with interests in test-time optimization and compositional generation.</li>
  <li><strong>Generative World Modeling:</strong> Scaling generative models to follow interactive instructions and integrate structured human knowledge for better prediction.</li>
  <li><strong>Video Understanding &amp; Reasoning:</strong> Leveraging foundations in action recognition and temporal grounding to interpret long-range event dynamics.</li>
</ul>

I am open to any positive communication and collaboration. Please feel free to contact me if you're interested!
